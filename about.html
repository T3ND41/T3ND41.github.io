About Hanekom Innovations

Wholly Zambian owned. Majority female owned. Supplying fit-for-purpose PPE and industrial products since 2003.

Our background

Formed in Kitwe, built on supply-chain experience

Hanekom Innovations Limited is a wholly Zambian-owned company located in Kitwe, with majority female ownership. It was formed on 19 May 2003 by a team of professionals with varied backgrounds and rich experience in domestic and international sales and marketing, and in supply chain.

We are a forward-thinking and well-established player in personal protective equipment to the mining, manufacturing, water utility, process, farming and mineral processing industries. Our expertise as an organisation lies in providing quality mining and industrial products and services at competitive prices, with efficient delivery in accordance with our clients' specifications and needs.

Our aim and objective is to provide the best personal protective equipment to our clients, because we believe there is no substitute for safety.
