# Photo Program Plan v2 — EVERY image on the site, worst-first

Supersedes the batch order in README.md section 6 (kept for history). Rules unchanged.
Owner directive: cover every image; restart from the beginning; work the really messed-up
images first, then ascend in quality; stick to the plan's rules.

## What stays fixed (the plan's rules — not negotiable)
- Fidelity first: no invented colours, materials, logos, stitching, hardware or model features.
- 5 views per product SKU (front / back / left / right / top); wearables on mannequin where the view requires it.
- Catalog: 1:1, 1200x1200 WebP <200KB + 400x400 <40KB, background #FFFFFF. Gallery: natural ratio, longest edge <=1600 (preview <=600).
- Naming: `SKU_view-angle_status_version.ext`; verdicts APPROVED / APPROVED WITH MINOR CORRECTIONS / REQUIRES REGENERATION / REQUIRES BETTER REFERENCE IMAGES.
- Batches of 2–3; a batch must reach verdict before the next starts.
- Never overwrite original supplier photos; nothing enters the site without Hanekom's human approval.
