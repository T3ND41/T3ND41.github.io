Why Hanekom

PPE backed by practical experience

Hanekom Innovations Limited was formed on 19 May 2003 by a team with deep experience in domestic and international sales, marketing and supply chain. We are wholly Zambian owned, with majority female ownership, and we have spent two decades learning what actually survives a Zambian shift.

Our expertise lies in supplying quality mining and industrial products at competitive prices, delivered on time and to the client's specification — with minimum downtime in critical areas of production.

Specification first

We match the item to the hazard — cut level, filtration class, EN standard, attachment points — not just to the lowest unit price.

Two-office coverage

Kitwe on the Copperbelt and Lusaka, so Copperbelt mines and Lusaka process plants both get a local point of contact.

Fast, quoted response

Send product names, quantities and sizes by WhatsApp or email and get a written quotation back.

Bulk and contract supply

Kitted-out crews, shutdown campaigns and standing supply arrangements — talk to us about volume terms.
