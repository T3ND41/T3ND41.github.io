CASE STUDY 01 · BUSINESS PLATFORM

Hanekom Innovations Digital Platform

A product-led business website designed to move industrial buyers from discovery to quotation and conversation with less friction.

ProblemApproachDecisionsValueNext

The problem

An industrial supplier needs more than a digital brochure. Buyers must be able to discover the right product, understand what it is for, and move into a quotation conversation without getting lost in a catalogue.

Core challenge

Turn a large product catalogue into a practical buying journey: find → evaluate → quote → contact.

The approach

The platform was structured around customer intent rather than internal product organization. Search, product cards, product pages, quotation actions and WhatsApp entry points all support the same outcome: help a buyer identify what they need and ask for it clearly.

DISCOVERY

Searchable catalogue

Products can be found through names, categories and simple need-based terms.

CONVERSION

Quote-building workflow

Selected products move into a quotation process instead of forcing buyers to manually reconstruct an order.

COMMUNICATION

Direct conversation

WhatsApp links shorten the path between product interest and a real sales discussion.

RESPONSIVE UX

Mobile-first behavior

Navigation and interactions were designed to remain usable for customers browsing on phones.

Key design decisions

  • Use strong product categorization and search rather than expecting customers to know exact product codes.
  • Keep quotation actions close to product information.
  • Design contact paths around how customers already communicate.
  • Prioritize performance and clear hierarchy over decorative animation.
  • Keep the system ready for later CRM, database and automation integration.

Value created

The site connects presentation and sales workflow. Instead of ending at “view product,” the experience is designed to continue toward an actionable request that the business can follow up.

Next iteration

The next stage is operational integration: website enquiries and quotations should feed a lead system, notifications, follow-up scheduling and reporting. That turns the website from a customer-facing interface into the front end of a measurable sales system.
