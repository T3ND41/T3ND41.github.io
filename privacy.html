Privacy notice

What this website collects, what it does not, and what you can ask us to do about it. Written to meet the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia.

Last reviewed: September 2026. Hanekom Innovations Limited is the data controller.

The short version. This website sets no cookies, runs no advertising, has no login, and loads nothing from any other company's servers. The only personal information it collects is what you deliberately type into the quote or contact form, and that goes to our sales inbox to answer your enquiry. Nothing else.

1. Who we are

Hanekom Innovations Limited, a company registered in Zambia, of 1559 Independence Avenue, Kitwe, Copperbelt Province, with a second office at 218A Mbereshi Street, Nyumba Yanga, Lusaka.

For anything in this notice, contact sales@hanekom.co.zm or +260 975 956 570.

2. What we collect, and only when you send it

The quote form and the contact form ask for the following. Nothing on this website collects any of it unless you fill a form in and submit it.

WhatWhy we need it
Your nameTo address the quotation and know who we are speaking to
CompanyTo price correctly — trade, contract and bulk terms differ
Email addressTo send you the written quotation
Phone / WhatsApp numberTo reach you if the enquiry needs a question answered
Town or site locationTo quote delivery accurately
IndustryTo recommend the right specification for the hazard
Your requirement details and quote listTo prepare the quotation itself

We ask for no more than that. We do not ask for, and you should never send us, identity document numbers, bank or card details, or health information. If you include any of that in a message, we will delete it and reply asking you to resend without it.

3. Our lawful basis

You give us these details so that we can quote you, which is a step taken at your request before entering into a contract, and where required we rely on your consent, given by submitting the form. Both are lawful bases under section 30 of the Data Protection Act. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us, and we will stop.

4. What we do with it

We use it to prepare and send your quotation, to answer your questions about it, and to fulfil and deliver an order if you place one. We also keep it as a record of the transaction, which we are required to do for tax and accounting purposes.

We do not sell your details, rent them, or share them with anyone for marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list because you asked for a quote. If we ever want to send you something you did not ask for, we will ask you first.

We share your details outside the company only where we have to: with a courier or transporter, to deliver goods to you; with a manufacturer or supplier, where an item has to be sourced or a warranty claimed; and with our accountants, auditors, or a public authority where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become orders: two years, so that we can pick up a conversation where it left off and honour a price we previously indicated. Records relating to an actual order are kept for the period Zambian tax and company law requires, currently six years from the end of the financial year. After that they are deleted.

6. Cookies, tracking and third parties

This site sets no cookies at all, and there is nothing to consent to. There is no advertising network, no social media pixel, no session recording and no cross-site tracking.

The site also loads no resource from any third-party server. Every image, stylesheet, font and script comes from hanekom.co.zm itself. That is a deliberate choice: it means your IP address is not disclosed to any other company simply because you read this page.

Your quote list stays in your browser

When you add products to a quote list, that list is stored by your own browser, on your own device, using a feature called local storage. It is never transmitted to us and we cannot see it. It stays there until you clear the list, or clear your browser data. If you use the "copy link to this list" button, the list is encoded into a web address that you control and choose who to send it to — again, it does not reach us unless you submit the form.

Server logs

Our web host records ordinary technical information for every visit — IP address, time, page requested, browser type. This is standard for any website and is used to keep the site running and to detect abuse. We do not use these logs to build a profile of you.

If you contact us by WhatsApp

The WhatsApp buttons on this site open WhatsApp with a message ready to send. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, not by us, and their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy. If you would rather not use it, email or telephone us instead — both reach the same team.

7. Where your information is held

Enquiries sent through this site arrive in our email inbox and are worked on from our offices in Kitwe and Lusaka. The Data Protection Act restricts transferring personal data outside Zambia, and where any part of our email or hosting arrangement involves a service operated outside the country we take that obligation seriously and keep the arrangement under review. If you would prefer your enquiry never to leave Zambia, telephone or WhatsApp us instead of using the form and we will take the details verbally.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete what we hold, where we are not required to keep it
  • Stop or restrict what we are doing with it
  • Give it to you in a portable format, or send it to someone else
  • Object to a particular use

Email sales@hanekom.co.zm with the subject line "Data request". We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled it, you may complain to the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia at dataprotection.gov.zm. We would rather you came to us first, but it is your right either way.

9. Keeping it safe

The site is served only over an encrypted connection, so what you type into a form cannot be read in transit. It carries a strict content security policy, which means a browser will refuse to run any script that is not part of the site — the control that would stop an attacker who compromised the site from capturing what you type. There is no database and no administrator login on this website to break into.

If you believe you have found a security problem with this site, please tell us at sales@hanekom.co.zm. We will not take legal action against anyone who reports a genuine flaw to us in good faith and gives us a reasonable opportunity to fix it.

10. Children

This is a business-to-business site for industrial buyers. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this notice we will change the review date at the top. Where the change is significant we will say so on the home page.
