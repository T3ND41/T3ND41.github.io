Quick reference

Standards you will see on our products

These are the designations that appear on the items in our catalogue. Confirm the exact certification on the supplied product before you order — certification is a property of the specific model, not the brand.

StandardApplies toWhat it covers
EN ISO 20345Safety footwearAll classes require a 200 joule toecap. Class letters (S1, S1P, S3, S5…) add penetration resistance, antistatic properties, energy absorption, water resistance and a cleated outsole.
ZS ISO 20345Safety footwear in ZambiaThe Zambian adoption. Safety footwear is a compulsory standard under Zambian law.
EN 388Gloves — mechanical risksSix characters: abrasion, coupe cut, tear, puncture, TDM cut (A–F) and impact (P).
EN 407Gloves — thermalHeat and flame performance, for welding and hot-work gloves.
EN ISO 374Gloves — chemicalPermeation performance against specified chemicals; type A, B or C.
EN 397Industrial helmetsShock absorption, penetration, flame resistance and chin-strap anchorage that releases between 150 N and 250 N.
EN ISO 20471High visibility clothingClass 1, 2 or 3 by the area of fluorescent and retroreflective material on the garment as worn.
EN 149Disposable respiratorsFFP1, FFP2 and FFP3 filtering facepiece classes for particulates.
EN 361Full body harnessesThe only acceptable body-holding device in a fall-arrest system. Attachment points marked A.
EN 355Energy absorbersLimits the force transmitted to the body during a fall arrest.
EN 358Work positioningBelts and lanyards for support at height — not a fall arrest system on their own.
EN 360Retractable fall arrestersSelf-retracting lifelines — webbing or wire rope.
EN 813Sit harnessesRope access and suspended work, used together with EN 361 fall arrest.
Type 5 / Type 6Disposable coverallsType 5: airborne solid particulates. Type 6: limited liquid splash.

Some Southern African manufactured PPE is certified to the SANS adoption of these standards and approved by the NRCS. That is equivalent in substance — but always quote what the actual specification sheet says rather than assuming an edition.
